# Jetsam Gossip

**Flotsam:** Items or data that float freely, typically as a result of being cast off unintentionally or as debris. In this project, flotsam refers to incidental or ambient data circulating in the system, not deliberately propagated.

**Jetsam:** Items or data that are deliberately jettisoned or shared, often to lighten a load or to disseminate information. In this project, jetsam refers to data or knowledge intentionally gossiped or broadcast to other nodes or agents.
